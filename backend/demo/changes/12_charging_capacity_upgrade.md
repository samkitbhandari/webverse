# Charging Infrastructure Upgrade - Whitefield Corridor

## Upgrade

Charging Hub North charging power is 350 kW per bay following the transformer
replacement.

## Operational effect

Whitefield Corridor utilisation is 84 percent at peak under the revised
timetable.

Fleet Deployment Phase 2 capacity is 300 vehicles under the upgraded charging
plan.

## Dependency

Fleet Deployment Phase 2 depends on Charging Hub North.

There is a risk of grid connection delay if the transformer upgrade is not
energised before the winter peak.
