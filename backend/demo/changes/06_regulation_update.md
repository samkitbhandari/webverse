# UNECE R100 Revision 3 - Regulatory Notice

## Summary

UNECE R100 rev.3 was published and reduced the battery operating limit from
70 degC to 60 degC for urban passenger service.

## Applicability

Battery Pack BP-7 must remain below 60 degC during continuous discharge.

UNECE R100 rev.3 affects Type Certification and Battery Pack BP-7.

## Transition

Type Certification submissions after the transition date must demonstrate
compliance against the revised envelope.

There is a critical risk of certification rejection if Thermal Architecture A7
is submitted unchanged.
