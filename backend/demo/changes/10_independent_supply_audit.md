# Independent Supply Chain Audit - Volta Cells Ltd

## Method

An independent audit of the Volta Cells Ltd plant was carried out against the
current supply agreement.

## Findings

Volta Cells Ltd capacity is 50000 units per month, confirming the figure in the
supply agreement.

Volta Cells Ltd lead time is 14 days, unchanged from the contracted term.

Volta Cells Ltd remains certified against the cell qualification standard.

## Conclusion

The audit found no discrepancy between the contracted terms and observed plant
throughput.
