# Battery Thermal Design Specification - Revision 4

## Scope

This specification governs the traction battery for the Helios shuttle platform.
Battery Pack BP-7 is the traction battery fitted to Fleet Deployment Phase 2.

## Thermal envelope

Battery Pack BP-7 must remain below 70 degC during continuous discharge.

Battery Pack BP-7 energy density is 178 Wh.

Thermal Architecture A7 implements the cooling approach for Battery Pack BP-7.

## Cooling architecture

Thermal Architecture A7 is a passive cooling design with no active coolant loop.

We have selected Thermal Architecture A7 because it removes 42 kg of mass per
vehicle and avoids a coolant maintenance interval.

Thermal Architecture A7 unit cost is 61000 INR.

## Validation

Measured pack temperature reached 68 degC during the summer duty cycle test.

Thermal Architecture A7 depends on ambient temperature remaining below 38 degC.

## Risk register

There is a critical risk of thermal runaway if sustained ambient temperature
exceeds the passive cooling envelope.
