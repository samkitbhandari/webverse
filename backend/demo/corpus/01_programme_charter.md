# Helios Urban Mobility Programme - Charter

## Programme scope

Helios Urban Mobility Programme delivers an autonomous electric shuttle service
across the Whitefield corridor. Fleet Deployment Phase 2 is the second rollout
stage and covers 240 vehicles.

Helios Urban Mobility Programme is owned by Meridian Transit Authority.

## Budget

Total programme budget must not exceed 480 crore.

Fleet Deployment Phase 2 capital cost is 214 crore.

## Schedule

Fleet Deployment Phase 2 is scheduled for revenue service in 9 months.

Type Certification depends on Thermal Architecture A7.

Fleet Deployment Phase 2 depends on Type Certification.

## Governance

We have decided to proceed with Fleet Deployment Phase 2 because the Whitefield
corridor pilot met all availability targets.

Meridian Transit Authority requires that service availability must be at least
99 percent across the operating day.
