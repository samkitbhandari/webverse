# Cell Supply Agreement - Volta Cells Ltd

## Parties

Volta Cells Ltd supplies lithium iron phosphate cells for Battery Pack BP-7.

Battery Pack BP-7 is owned by Helios Urban Mobility Programme.

## Commercial terms

Volta Cells Ltd capacity is 50000 units per month.

Volta Cells Ltd lead time is 14 days.

Volta Cells Ltd unit cost is 3120 INR.

## Selection

We have selected Volta Cells Ltd because their lead time is the shortest of the
three qualified vendors and their cells meet the thermal envelope.

## Alternative vendor

Kirin Energy Systems capacity is 34000 units per month.

Kirin Energy Systems lead time is 31 days.

Kirin Energy Systems unit cost is 2740 INR.

Kirin Energy Systems cells are rated for continuous operation below 60 degC.

## Continuity

Fleet Deployment Phase 2 depends on Volta Cells Ltd.

There is a risk of production stoppage if a single supplier holds the entire
cell allocation.
